# A guest

A guest's contact details, notes and all their stays.

## Common tasks

**Change details:** choose **Edit**, change name, email, phone or notes, and save.

**Erase a guest's data** (when they ask for it)

1. Choose **Erase personal data**.
2. Name, email, phone and notes are removed. The bookings stay, anonymised, because they are accounting records.

## What if …

- **… a guest is there twice?** Use the search in the booking form next time; each email address belongs to one guest.
