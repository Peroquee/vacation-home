# Bookings

All stays, with guest, dates, status and amount. The open **Requests** from your website are at the top.

## Common tasks

**Find a booking**

1. Choose **Upcoming**, **Past** or **All** at the top.
2. Search by the guest's name or email, or filter by home and status.
3. Tap a row to open the booking.

**Add a booking by hand** (a phone call, say)

1. Choose **New booking**.
2. Fill in the form and save.

## What the status means

- **Inquiry:** a wish, not yet agreed. Holds no days.
- **Reserved:** accepted, the days are held. The deposit hasn't arrived.
- **Confirmed:** the deposit is in; the rest follows.
- **Paid:** everything is paid.
- **Expired:** not paid in time, or not answered. The days are free again.
- **Cancelled:** called off.

## What if …

- **… a request isn't listed here?** Requests from the website are under **Requests** (at the top) until you accept or decline them.
