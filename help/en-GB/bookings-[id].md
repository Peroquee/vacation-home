# A booking

Everything about one stay: guest, dates, the price line by line, payments and what is due next.

## Common tasks

**Record a payment** (the deposit, say)

1. Check on your bank account that the money is there.
2. Under **Payments**, choose **Record payment**.
3. Pick the kind (deposit, balance), check the amount and date, and save.
4. The status moves on by itself: to **Confirmed** after the deposit, to **Paid** once everything is in.
   :::if-email
   The guest gets an email each time.
   :::
   :::if-no-email
   Let the guest know the money has arrived: emails aren't set up yet.
   :::

**Extend a deadline**

1. Under **Payment**, choose **Change deadlines**.
2. Set the new date and save.

**Send the guest their booking page**

Choose **Guest link**. It shows the guest what to pay, your bank details and their booking.

**Cancel a booking**

1. Choose **Cancel booking**.
2. Say **who is cancelling**: the guest or you. The app suggests the refund under your terms and today's date (with a schedule, it says how much of the deposit is kept today); you can change it.
3. Send the refund, then record it under **Record payment** as a refund.

## What the price lines mean

- **Nights × rate:** a line per season.
- **Discount:** for a longer stay.
- **Cleaning fee.**
- **Kurtaxe:** for the municipality, not your income.

## What if …

- **… the deposit hasn't arrived?** Wait until the deadline, ask the guest, or extend the deadline. Without a payment the reservation expires and the days are freed.
- **… the guest paid too much?** Record what actually came in, and give back the rest as a **refund**.
