# Categories

The groups for your income and expenses, such as "Cleaning" or "Insurance".

## Common tasks

- **Add a category:** **Add category**, then name, kind and colour.
- **Rename it or change its colour:** **Edit** on the category.
- **Delete a category:** if it is in use, the app asks where its transactions should go.

## Good to know

The default categories can be renamed or hidden, but not deleted: the app needs them, for booking income and the Kurtaxe for instance.
