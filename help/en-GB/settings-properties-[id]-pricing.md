# Prices

Everything that sets a home's price. A stay is priced like this: night by night by its season, then the discount comes off the nights, then the cleaning fee is added and, if set up, the Kurtaxe.

## Common tasks

**Add a season** (summer high season, say)

1. Under **Seasonal prices**, choose **Add season**.
2. Enter a name, the first and last day and the nightly rate. Leave **Every year** ticked if it comes round every year.
3. Save. The strip below shows the next 12 months.

**A discount for longer stays**

1. Choose **Edit discounts** and **Add tier**.
2. For example: from 7 nights 10% off. Save.

**Set up the Kurtaxe**

1. Choose **Edit Kurtaxe** and tick **Charge a Kurtaxe**.
2. Enter the rates from your municipality's statute: per adult and night, and age groups for children.
3. Choose whether your prices **include** it or it is **added**.

## Good to know

- **Existing bookings keep their price** when you change anything here.
- Guests see the season names in the price: choose names for guests.
- The standard rate and the cleaning fee are changed on the home itself, under **Edit**.
