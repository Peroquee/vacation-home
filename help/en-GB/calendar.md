# Calendar

See at a glance when your homes are taken: bookings, reserved requests and blocked days.

## Common tasks

**Add a booking**

1. Drag across the calendar from arrival to departure (or tap the first and the last day).
2. Choose **New booking**. The form already has the dates.
3. Pick the guest, check the price and save.

**Block days** (for repairs or a family visit)

1. Mark the days as above.
2. Choose **Block dates** and add a reason if you like.

**Look at a booking**

Tap the booking's bar: its main details open, with a link to the whole booking.

## What you see

- **A solid bar in the home's colour:** a booking that holds the days. With "reserved, awaiting payment" next to it, the deposit hasn't arrived yet.
- **Light with a dashed outline:** a request you haven't accepted. It holds no days.
- **Grey stripes:** blocked days, also those from booking platforms or Google Calendar.
- **A red notice at the top:** two bookings overlap. Sort it out soon.

## What if …

- **… a booking from Airbnb or Booking.com is missing?** The platforms are checked regularly. Under **Settings → Booking platforms (iCal)**, check that the calendar is there and when it was last checked.
- **… I can't mark the days?** A booking or block is already there. A request you haven't accepted blocks nothing.
