# Dashboard

Your key figures for a period: revenue, expenses, profit, occupancy and the next bookings.

## Common tasks

- **Pick a period:** at the top under **Period**, e.g. "Year to date".
- **Look at one home:** choose it under **Property**.
- **A report for your tax adviser:** **Export** → Financial report (PDF), or the transactions as CSV.

## What the figures mean

- **Revenue:** what you have taken in, without the Kurtaxe.
- **Occupancy rate:** booked nights divided by available nights. Blocked days don't count as available.
- The small arrows compare with the same period a year earlier.
