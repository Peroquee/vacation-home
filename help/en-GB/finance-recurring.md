# Recurring costs

Costs that come round regularly: mortgage, insurance, internet. The app enters them in your finances on every due date.

You find them under **Finance → Recurring costs**, the tab at the top of every Finance page.

## Common tasks

1. Choose **Add a recurring cost**.
2. Enter the amount, category, how often (monthly, quarterly, yearly) and the first due date.
3. Save. From the start date on, the transaction appears by itself.

**Pause or end one:** **Edit** on the cost. Transactions already entered stay.

**From an expense:** when you enter an expense, the window asks **Does this expense come back regularly?**. **Make recurring** takes the amount, category, date and description into a new recurring cost that runs from that date.

**Keep the contract or policy:** **Edit**, then **Add documents** under **Documents**: the loan agreement, the insurance policy or the yearly statement, say (PDF or photo, up to 10 MB each, up to 20).

**One month's invoice:** belongs on that month's payment. Under **Payments** in the same window you see every payment so far, whether it has a receipt yet, and the paperclip adds one.

## What the status means

- **Active:** being entered.
- **Paused:** not for now.
- **Ended:** its end date has passed.

## Good to know

- **The paperclip** in the list shows that a cost has documents.
- **Deleting** a cost also removes its own documents. Its payments stay in the ledger, with their receipts.
