# Recurring costs

Costs that come round regularly: mortgage, insurance, internet. The app enters them in your finances on every due date.

## Common tasks

1. Choose **Add a recurring cost**.
2. Enter the amount, category, how often (monthly, quarterly, yearly) and the first due date.
3. Save. From the start date on, the transaction appears by itself.

**Pause or end one:** **Edit** on the cost. Transactions already entered stay.

## What the status means

- **Active:** being entered.
- **Paused:** not for now.
- **Ended:** its end date has passed.
