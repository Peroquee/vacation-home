# Requests

Requests from your public booking site arrive here. Each waits for your answer until the deadline shown.

## Common tasks

**Accept a request**

1. Read the request: dates, guests, message.
2. Choose **Accept and reserve**.
3. The days are now held for the guest.
   :::if-email
   The guest gets an email with the deposit and your bank details.
   :::
   :::if-no-email
   Send the guest the **Guest link** from the booking: it shows the deposit and your bank details. (Emails aren't set up yet.)
   :::

**Decline a request**

1. Choose **Decline** and, if you like, a short reason.
2. The days stay free.
   :::if-email
   The guest is told by email.
   :::
   :::if-no-email
   Let the guest know yourself: emails aren't set up yet.
   :::

**Keep an eye on payments**

Below the requests are the reserved bookings still waiting for money, with their deadline.

## What if …

- **… I can't accept a request?** The days have been taken since. Write to the guest, or decline.
- **… I don't answer in time?** The request expires after the deadline (set under **Settings → Booking requests**).
  :::if-email
  The guest then gets an email.
  :::
- **… the deposit doesn't arrive?** When the deadline passes, the reservation ends and the days are free again. You can extend the deadline in the booking first, under **Change deadlines**.
