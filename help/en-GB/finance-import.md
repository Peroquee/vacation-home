# Import transactions

Bring in many transactions at once from a CSV file, such as a bank statement.

## How to

1. Upload the file.
2. Match the columns: date, amount, description.
3. Check the preview. The app spots transactions you already have and leaves them out.
4. Choose category and home, and import.

## What if …

- **… the amounts look wrong?** Check whether the file uses a comma or a point for decimals, and choose the matching format.
- **… a row with a negative income is refused?** That is a refund. Refunds are recorded on their booking (**Record payment**), not imported.
