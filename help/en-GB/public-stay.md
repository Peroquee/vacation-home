# How booking works

1. **Send a request:** pick your dates and send us a request. It costs nothing and doesn't commit you to anything yet.
2. **Our answer:** we reply {requestAnswer}. If we accept, the dates are reserved for you, and you get a link to your own booking page.
3. **Deposit:** you transfer the deposit of {deposit} {depositDue} of our acceptance. That confirms your booking.
4. **Balance:** you transfer the rest {balanceDue}.
5. **Confirmation:** once everything has arrived, we send you the exact address and everything you need for your arrival.

## When are the dates held for me?

A request doesn't hold the dates yet: until we have answered, others can request them too.
If we haven't answered {requestAnswer}, your request lapses, and you're welcome to send a new one.

Once we accept, the dates are reserved for you until the deposit is due.
If the deposit hasn't arrived by the end of that day, the reservation lapses and the dates become free again.
Once the deposit has arrived, your booking is confirmed and the dates are yours.

If your arrival is at most {shortNotice} away when we accept, there is no deposit: you transfer the full amount {fullDue} of our acceptance.

## How do I pay?

You pay by bank transfer.
:::if-bank-details
Your booking page shows our bank details, the amount and the date it's due by.
:::
:::if-girocode
It also shows a GiroCode: scan it with your banking app, and the transfer is filled in for you.
:::
Please quote your booking reference, so we can match your payment to your booking.
