# New booking

For a booking that doesn't come through your website: a phone call, an email, regular guests.

## How to

1. Choose the **home** and the **guest** (or add a new one).
2. Enter **arrival** and **departure**. The form says straight away whether the days are free.
3. Enter the guests (adults and, if asked, children by age).
4. Check the price below: it comes from your seasonal prices, discounts and the cleaning fee.
5. Choose the **status** and save with **Add booking**.

## Good to know

- **Own nightly rate:** leave it empty and your seasonal prices apply. A rate here prices every night the same.
- **Set the total by hand:** for a negotiated total.
- **Warnings about booking rules** (a minimum stay, say) are only hints: you may book anyway.
