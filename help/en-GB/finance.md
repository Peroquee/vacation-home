# Finance

Your income and expenses, month by month. Confirmed bookings appear here by themselves (switch: **Settings → Booking income**), and a booking with payments recorded appears with its payments.

## Common tasks

**Enter an expense** (a tradesman's bill, say)

1. Choose **Add a transaction** (or press **T**).
2. Choose **Expense**, the amount, category, date and home.
3. Attach the receipt straight away if you like: **Add receipts**, drag the file onto the window, or on a phone **Take a photo**. Then save.

The receipts upload once the transaction is saved. If a file doesn't make it, the transaction stays saved all the same: the window says which file it was, and **Try again** uploads just that one again.

**Bring in bank statements**

Choose **Import CSV** and follow the steps.

**Find missing receipts**

Under **Receipts** above the list, choose **Without a receipt**, then **Apply**: the list shows only the transactions without one, after a CSV import for instance. Open a transaction to attach its receipt.

**Export for your tax return**

Choose **Export CSV**. For Excel in German, take the variant with semicolons.

## Good to know

- **Kurtaxe (durchlaufend):** the money for the municipality. It is listed, but never counts as revenue. Its overview is under **Kurtaxe**.
- **The paperclip** in the list shows that a transaction has receipts.
- **Recurring costs** (mortgage, insurance) enter themselves on their due date.

## What if …

- **… a payment appears twice?** Check whether it was recorded on the booking and also entered here by hand, and delete the hand-made entry.
