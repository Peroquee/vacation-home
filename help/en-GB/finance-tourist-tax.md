# Kurtaxe

The Kurtaxe belongs to the municipality: you collect it and pass it on. This page shows, month by month, what the stays owe and what has come in for it.

## What the columns mean

- **Stays:** booked stays arriving in that month.
- **Owed:** the Kurtaxe of those stays.
- **Collected:** what has actually been paid for it (less refunds).

## Good to know

The page counts by **the month of arrival**. Ask your municipality how they want it reported (by arrival, nights or payment) before you file.

## What if …

- **… a guest is exempt?** In the booking, use **Set the Kurtaxe by hand**, e.g. €0, with a reason.
