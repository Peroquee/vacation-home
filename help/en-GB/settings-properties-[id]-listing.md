# Listing

What guests see on your home's public page.

## Common tasks

**Add photos**

1. Choose **Add photos**, straight from your phone too.
2. The first photo is the cover. The arrows change the order.
3. Give each photo a short description: it helps people who can't see it.

**Write the texts:** title, description and house rules, in German and English. Where one language is missing, the other is shown.

**Set arrival and departure times:** under **Arrival and departure**, the earliest arrival, the earliest departure and the latest departure, each optional. Guests see them on the public page (in its help too), on their booking page and in the emails, for example "Arrival from 15:00 · Departure between 08:00 and 10:00".

## Good to know

- The exact address is **never** on the public page; guests get it only with the confirmation once they have paid in full.
- Photos are stored without location or camera data.
- The times are in the property's timezone. A booking keeps the times it was reserved or made under: changing them here only affects new bookings.
