# What happens now

:::if-inquiry
We have received your request and will reply by {answerBy}.
Your dates aren't reserved yet, and there's nothing to pay yet. If we accept, this page shows what to pay and by when.
:::
:::if-reserved
We have accepted your request: the dates are reserved for you.
**Your next step:** transfer {nextAmount} by {nextDate}. If it hasn't arrived by the end of that day, the reservation lapses and the dates become free again.
:::
:::if-confirmed
Your deposit has arrived: your booking is confirmed and the dates are yours.
**Your next step:** transfer the balance of {nextAmount} by {nextDate}.
Once everything has arrived, we send you the exact address and everything you need for your arrival.
:::
:::if-paid
Everything is paid: thank you! Your booking is complete.
We send you the exact address and everything you need for your arrival. If you haven't received it, just write to us.
:::
:::if-declined
We're sorry: we couldn't accept your request for these dates. Nothing has been charged.
You're welcome to request other dates.
:::
:::if-request-expired
We couldn't answer your request in time, so it has lapsed. Nothing has been charged.
You're welcome to send a new request.
:::
:::if-reservation-expired
The payment didn't arrive in time, so the reservation has lapsed and the dates are free again.
If you'd still like to come, send us a new request or write to us.
:::
:::if-cancelled
This booking has been cancelled. If a refund is due, we transfer it to you under the cancellation terms you agreed to.
:::

## How do I pay?

:::if-transfer
By bank transfer to the account shown on this page, for the amount shown there.
Please quote **{remittance}** as the payment reference, so we can match your payment to your booking.
:::
:::if-girocode
Or scan the GiroCode with your banking app: it fills in the transfer for you.
:::
:::if-transfer
We record your payment when it arrives, and this page then shows it. A bank transfer can take one or two working days.
:::

## When do I get the exact address?

:::if-holds
We send it to you with the confirmation, once everything is paid.
:::

## How do I add the stay to my calendar?

:::if-holds
Choose **Add to calendar (.ics)** on this page: the file works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and most other calendar apps. Google Calendar and Outlook.com have their own buttons.
If your booking changes, opening the file again updates the entry instead of adding a second one.
:::

## What if I need to cancel?

:::if-inquiry
You can withdraw your request at any time: just let us know. Nothing has been charged.
:::
:::if-holds
The cancellation policy on this page says what is refunded if you cancel today.
To cancel, just write to us. We confirm the cancellation and transfer any refund to you.
:::

## How do I reach you?

:::if-reference
Write to us at [{contact}](mailto:{contact}), quoting your booking reference {reference}.
:::
:::if-no-reference
Write to us at [{contact}](mailto:{contact}).
:::

## What happens to my data?

We use your details only for your request and your booking. This page sets no cookies and uses no tracking.
You'll find everything else in our [privacy policy](/stay/legal/privacy).
