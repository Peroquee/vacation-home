# Edit a booking

Change a booking's dates, guests, price or notes.

## Good to know

- **The agreed price stays** as long as you only change the guest, notes or status. It is worked out anew only when you change the dates, the own nightly rate or the cleaning fee.
- **Change the number of guests,** and only the Kurtaxe is worked out anew.
- **Set the total by hand:** for a negotiated price. It then stays until you untick it.

## Common tasks

**Move the dates**

1. Change arrival and departure.
2. Check the new price below and save.

## What if …

- **… the new dates are taken?** The form shows it straight away. A confirmed booking can't be moved onto taken days.
